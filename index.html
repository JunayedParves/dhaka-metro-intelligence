Classification legend
ActualPublished figure from a government, JICA, audited or press source.
SyntheticSimulated record calibrated to published totals. Not an observation and not a DMTCL feed.

Metric definitions
Daily ridershipΣ station entries (boardings). Entries and exits are never added together.
Station throughputEntries + exits.
Capacity utilizationPassenger load on a train's busiest section ÷ 2,300 × 100.
Revenue per passengerTicket revenue ÷ passenger boardings.
Farebox recovery ratioFarebox revenue ÷ operating expenses × 100.
Travel-time savingBaseline road travel time − metro journey time (door to door).
Net CO₂ impactRoad CO₂ avoided − CO₂ from metro electricity (grid factor 0.70 kg/kWh, assumed).
Simulated-live valuesDay total × share of hourly demand elapsed at the simulation clock.
